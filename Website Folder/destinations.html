Popular Destinations

Beaches, hills, and heritage. Find the spot that calls you.

Chocolate Hills – Bohol

The Chocolate Hills are one of the most famous natural attractions in the Philippines. They consist of hundreds of cone-shaped hills that turn brown during the dry season. Visitors can enjoy breathtaking views and take memorable photos.

Location: Carmen, Bohol

Basilica del Santo Niño – Cebu City

The Basilica del Santo Niño is one of the oldest Roman Catholic churches in the Philippines. It houses the image of the Santo Niño, an important religious symbol in Cebu. It is a popular destination for pilgrims and tourists interested in history and religion.

Location: Cebu City, Cebu

Temple of Leah – Cebu City

The Temple of Leah is a grand Roman-inspired structure built as a symbol of love and remembrance. It features impressive architecture, statues, and elegant interiors. Visitors can enjoy its beautiful design and panoramic views of Cebu City.

Location: Cebu City, Cebu

Sirao Flower Garden – Cebu City

Sirao Flower Garden is a colorful attraction famous for its vibrant flowers and scenic mountain surroundings. It is often called the Little Amsterdam of Cebu because of its flower displays. It is a popular spot for sightseeing and photography.

Location: Cebu City, Cebu

TOPS – Cebu City

TOPS Cebu is a popular sightseeing destination located in the mountains of Busay. It offers a panoramic view of Cebu City and nearby islands. It is especially beautiful during sunset and nighttime when the city lights shine.

Location: Cebu City, Cebu

Baclayon Church – Bohol

Baclayon Church is one of the oldest stone churches in the Philippines. It showcases Spanish colonial architecture and preserves important religious and historical artifacts. It is a meaningful destination for visitors interested in Bohol's history and culture.

Location: Baclayon, Bohol

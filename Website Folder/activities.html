Activities & Experiences

Fire, forest, and flavor. Pick your kind of adventure.

Adventure

  • Canyoneering: Explore rivers, swim in natural pools, and jump into waterfalls in Badian, Cebu.
  • Sardine Run: Swim alongside thousands of sardines in the clear waters of Moalboal, Cebu.
  • River Cruising: Enjoy a relaxing boat ride while admiring the lush scenery of Loboc River, Bohol.

Culture

  • Sinulog Festival: Celebrate Cebu's religious heritage through colorful street dancing and performances honoring the Santo Niño.
  • Sandugo Festival: Experience Bohol's history through cultural performances commemorating the historic blood compact.
  • Church Heritage Tour: Explore centuries-old churches and Spanish colonial architecture in Baclayon, Bohol.

Food

  • Church Heritage Tour: Explore centuries-old churches and Spanish colonial architecture in Baclayon, Bohol.
  • Peanut Kisses: Enjoy crunchy peanut cookies inspired by the famous Chocolate Hills of Bohol.
  • Dried Mangoes: Try sweet and chewy dried mango slices, one of Cebu's most popular local treats.

Travel Tips

  • Plan Ahead: Book accommodations, ferry tickets, and tours before your trip.
  • Stay Protected: Bring sunscreen, water, comfortable clothes, and other travel essentials.
  • Respect Local Culture: Follow local rules, respect religious sites, and keep tourist attractions clean.

Packing Checklist

  • Valid ID
  • Sunscreen
  • Swimwear and towel
  • Comfortable shoes
  • Power bank
  • Cash
